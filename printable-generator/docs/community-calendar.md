## Tips for Community Management
1. **Respond within 4 hours** to new member introductions
2. **Pin the best content** each week to the top
3. **Celebrate milestones** — 100 posts, 500 members, etc.
4. **Feature user content** in email newsletters
5. **Turn feature requests** into actual features (best marketing)
6. **Seasonal content** — adjust themes for Ramadan, Hajj, Eid, back-to-school
